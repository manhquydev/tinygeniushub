## Business Model (Decided)
- **Subscription:** 99k/month | 799k/year (7-day trial, 30-day money-back)
- **Premium Courses:** 299k–499k one-time (Sub users get 20% off)
- **Differentiation:** Math 2-6 = blue ocean; Certificate viral loop; Physical+Digital bundle (phase 2)
