# Phase 05: Go-To-Market Launch Playbook

## Context Links
- All research: Researchers A-D
- Phases 01-04: pricing, content, technical, growth
- Revenue target: 1,000-1,200 paying users Year 1

## Overview
- **Priority:** P1 (Critical)
- **Status:** Done — 2026-02-25
- **Description:** Execute phased GTM launch: pre-launch waitlist + beta, launch week content blitz, 90-day growth ramp, seasonal campaigns, and budget allocation for bootstrap operation.

## Key Insights
- Beta families provide testimonials + product feedback before public launch
- Launch week needs coordinated multi-channel push (not drip)
- 90-day ramp: 100 → 500 → 1,000 paying users is realistic with K>0.3
- Seasonal peaks: back-to-school (Aug-Sep) + Tet (Jan-Feb) = 2x signup spikes
- Bootstrap budget ~$400/mo total is viable with organic-first strategy

## KPI Dashboard
Track weekly in spreadsheet (migrate to dashboard later):
- **Acquisition:** signups, trial starts, source attribution (UTM)
- **Activation:** first lesson within 24h, aha moment by D3
- **Retention:** D7 return rate, D30 return rate
- **Revenue:** MRR, ARPU, trial-to-paid conversion
- **Referral:** K-factor, shares per user, referral conversion rate
- **Engagement:** lessons/week, streak length, report open rate

## Seasonal Campaign Calendar

### Back-to-School (Aug 15 - Sep 15)
- Theme: "Chuan bi cho be vao lop — bat dau tu hom nay"
- Offer: 20% off annual plan for new signups
- Content: 4 TikTok + 2 blog posts on school readiness
- KOL push: 10 coordinated posts

### Tet Campaign (Jan 10 - Feb 10)

## Success Criteria
- 100 paying users by Day 30
- 700 paying users by Day 90
- CAC < $3 average across all channels
- At least 1 channel achieving CAC < $1 (organic)
- K-factor > 0.2 by Day 30

## Risk Assessment
- Launch week flop if no coordinated push — mitigate with pre-scheduled posts
- Beta families may churn if product has bugs — fix top 3 issues before launch
- Budget too tight for paid ads — rely on organic, scale ads only with proven ROAS
- Seasonal campaigns need 6-week prep — calendar reminders mandatory
