## Facebook Strategy

**Landscape:**
- Vietnam: ~70M Facebook users, ~90% of internet users active on platform
- "Mẹ Bỉm Sữa" groups: 500K–2M members each; dozens of variants exist nationally + by city
- Key groups: "Hội Mẹ Bỉm Sữa", "Nuôi Dạy Con Khoa Học", "Mẹ Và Bé" — combined reach 10M+
- Core demographic 25–38 female, exactly target audience

**Ad rules in groups:**
- Most groups ban direct ads; require admin approval or paid pinned posts
- Organic seeding via "soft content" (tips, questions, reviews) outperforms hard ads
- Admin collaboration: 2M–10M VND/post in large groups ($80–$400)

**Best tactics:**
- Post relatable parenting content with subtle product mention
- Seed genuine parent testimonials via nano-KOLs in groups
- Facebook Ads: target women 25–38, parenting interests, city-level for premium pricing
- Retargeting pixel on website for conversion campaigns

## Zalo OA Strategy

**Reach:**
- 74–75M monthly active users in Vietnam (2024); ~60% of population
- 2nd most used app after Facebook in VN; dominant for 30–50 age group
- ZNS (Zalo Notification Service): transactional messages with ~85–90% open rate

**EdTech use cases:**
- Broadcast messages to followers: lesson reminders, new content alerts
- ZNS for payment confirmations, trial expiry, re-engagement
- Chatbot for FAQ, onboarding flow, free trial activation
- Community mini-groups within Zalo for cohort-based learning

**Best practices:**
- Build OA follower base via QR code at signup + Facebook cross-promotion
- ZNS: personalize with child's name, age — significantly boosts CTR
- Broadcast cadence: max 2–3/week; avoid spam or users block OA
- Free tier: 100 ZNS/day; paid ZNS ~200–500 VND/message ($0.008–$0.02)

## TikTok / Short Video

**Vietnam stats:**
- 50M+ TikTok users; top 5 globally by per-user engagement time (~90 min/day)
- Parenting content category growing 40%+ YoY
- Algorithm favors high-completion-rate short clips (15–45 sec) and saves

**What goes viral (parenting niche):**
- "Con tôi học tiếng Anh như thế nào" (how my child learns English) — POV parent content
- Before/after transformation: toddler speaking English after 30 days
- Day-in-the-life: bilingual family routines
- Reaction videos: child reacting to learning app
- "Mẹo dạy con" (parenting tips) — practical, fast-paced

**High-performing hashtags:**
- `#nuoidaycon` — parenting tips (100M+ views)
- `#mamom` / `#mebimsuaviet` — Vietnamese mom community
- `#dayconhoc` — teaching children
- `#trememhoctienganh` — kids learning English
- `#giaoducsom` — early education
- `#lamemeviet` — Vietnamese mom lifestyle

**Content formats:**
- Hook in first 2 seconds; show child on screen (high engagement)
- Text overlay summarizing tip (for muted viewing)
- Duet/stitch with trending parenting content
- TikTok LIVE for Q&A with parents: drives follows + trust

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nano | 1K–10K | 500K–2M | $20–80 | 5–10% |
| Micro | 10K–100K | 2M–15M | $80–600 | 3–8% |
| Mid | 100K–500K | 15M–50M | $600–2,000 | 1–3% |
| Macro | 500K–1M | 50M–150M | $2,000–6,000 | 0.5–2% |
| Mega | 1M+ | 150M–500M+ | $6,000–20,000+ | 0.3–1% |

**Recommended approach for EdTech startup:**
- **Phase 1:** Seed 20–30 nano/micro KOLs (total budget $1,500–3,000/month)
  - Higher trust, authentic content, lower fraud risk
  - Target: "bilingual mom" KOLs, "teacher-mom" KOLs
- **Phase 2:** 2–3 mid-tier KOLs for awareness spikes ($1,200–6,000/campaign)
- ROI expectation: micro-KOL campaigns typically yield 3–8x ROAS in EdTech apps if trial CTA is clear

**Notable KOL categories to target:**
- "EduMom" TikTok: moms documenting child's learning journey
- Bilingual family channels on YouTube
- Kindergarten/preschool teachers with TikTok following
- Pediatric development specialists (credibility signal)

## Email Marketing

**SEA/Vietnam EdTech benchmarks (2024):**
- Open rate: 28–35% (education sector outperforms global avg of 21–25%)
- Click-through rate: 3–5%
- Unsubscribe rate: <0.5% if content is relevant
- Apple MPP inflates open rates; use CTR as primary KPI

**Vietnam-specific notes:**
- Email adoption lower than Zalo among general population; use email for premium/paying users
- Best for: onboarding sequences, weekly progress reports (personalized per child), re-engagement
- Gmail dominates; optimize for mobile (85%+ open on mobile in VN)
- Subject lines in Vietnamese outperform English for local audience

**Recommended stack:**
- Brevo (ex-Sendinblue) or Mailchimp — both have reasonable free tiers
- Combine with Zalo ZNS: email for content-rich updates, Zalo for time-sensitive alerts
