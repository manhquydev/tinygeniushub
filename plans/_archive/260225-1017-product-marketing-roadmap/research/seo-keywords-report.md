## Keyword Clusters → Blog Topic Buckets

### Cluster A: English Learning for Toddlers
Keywords: #1, #2, #3, #4, #13, #16, #19
- High vol, high competition — need strong content quality
- Parent intent: "how to teach" + "which app is best"

### Cluster B: Math for Preschoolers
Keywords: #5, #6, #7, #8, #14, #15
- Medium vol, moderate competition — winnable with long-tail
- Parent intent: methods, games, age-specific advice

### Cluster C: Educational Apps (General)
Keywords: #9, #10, #11, #12, #18
- Lower vol but high purchase intent — conversion-focused
- Parent intent: compare/choose app, safe for kids

### Cluster D: Early Childhood Education (Thought Leadership)
Keywords: #17, #20
- Low vol but high trust-building value
- Parent intent: "am I doing the right thing?"
