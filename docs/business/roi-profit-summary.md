## 💡 Key Recommendations

### ✅ DO
1. **Focus on Standard & Premium tiers** - Best margin/volume balance
2. **Upsell from Lite** - Push users to higher tiers quickly
3. **Build TikTok presence** - Lowest CAC ($5-10)
4. **Offer annual plans** - 20% discount for better cash flow
5. **Target families/homeschool groups** - Highest profit per user

### ⚠️ AVOID
1. **Don't compete on Lite tier** - Margin too thin (3%)
2. **Don't ignore churn** - Target <10% monthly
3. **Don't overspend on Facebook Ads** - CAC too high ($8-15)

## 🎓 Lessons from Analysis

### What Makes This Business Attractive
1. **High gross margins** (80%+) - Digital content business
2. **Low marginal costs** - Same content serves unlimited users
3. **Predictable revenue** - Subscription model
4. **Scalable infrastructure** - CDN handles growth

### Key Risks to Monitor
1. **Content licensing** - Abeka license costs may increase
2. **CDN costs at scale** - Bandwidth costs grow with users
3. **Competition** - Other EdTech platforms entering market
4. **Churn rate** - Must keep <10% for healthy LTV
