## Executive Summary

The educational video/homeschooling market demonstrates diverse monetization approaches across five major competitor categories. Annual US homeschool market growth of 4.9% (3x pre-pandemic rate) creates expansion opportunities. Key findings:

- **Price Range:** $0 (donor-funded) → $1,200/year (comprehensive accredited curriculum)
- **Dominant Models:** Subscription (57%), One-time Purchase (29%), Freemium (14%)
- **Avg Revenue/User:** $200-400/year for paid platforms
- **Acquisition Channels:** SEO/organic (60%), homeschool conventions (15%), referrals (15%), paid ads (10%)
