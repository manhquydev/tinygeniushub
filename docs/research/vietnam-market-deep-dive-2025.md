- Rural families focus on public school + basic supplementary materials

### 2.2 Parent Priorities & Psychology

**Investment Mindset:**
- "Education is THE investment" - parents willing to borrow for children's education
- Multi-generational support: grandparents often contribute to education funds
- High-stakes exams drive spending (THPT QG - national high school exam)

**Key Pain Points:**
1. **Financial pressure**: Tuition fees doubled in recent years
2. **Quality concerns**: Searching for credible, results-driven platforms
3. **Time constraints**: Busy parents need self-paced, automated learning
4. **Teacher shortage**: 120,000 unfilled teaching positions (May 2025)
5. **"Empty heart" syndrome**: Children achieving high grades but lacking life motivation

### 2.3 Decision-Making Factors

**Top 5 Platform Selection Criteria:**
1. **Proven results** (test scores, university admissions)
2. **Qualified teachers** (credentials, experience)
3. **Affordability** (value for money vs competitors)
4. **Convenience** (mobile-first, on-demand)
5. **Social proof** (peer recommendations, reviews)

## 3. Payment Trends in Vietnam EdTech

### 3.1 Payment Method Landscape

**Vietnam Payments Market 2024:**
- Total value: **USD 21.9 billion**
- 2032 forecast: **USD 42.8 billion**
- QR payment CAGR: **14.27%** (2025-2032)

### 3.2 Top Payment Methods

| Method | Market Position | Key Stats |
|--------|----------------|-----------|
| **MoMo** | Market leader | 40M+ active users, largest merchant network |
| **ZaloPay** | #2 position | Integrated with Zalo (Vietnam's top messaging app), 12,000+ chain stores |
| **VietQR** | National standard | Interoperable across wallets/banks, government-backed |
| **VNPay** | Major player | Strong bank integrations |
| **Credit Cards** | Growing | <10% of transactions, mainly urban/high-income |

### 3.3 EdTech Payment Preferences

**Why QR/Wallets Dominate:**
1. **60%+ prefer QR** for cashless transactions (Visa, 2025)
2. **Low barrier**: Works on basic smartphones
3. **Trust**: MoMo = "safe" brand in Vietnamese consumer mind
4. **Government push**: VietQR interoperability reduces fragmentation

**Merchant Considerations:**
- QR fees lower than card transactions
- Faster settlement (real-time for major wallets)
- Must support **at least MoMo + ZaloPay** for market coverage
- VietQR = universal compatibility across all banking apps

### 3.4 Payment UX Best Practices for EdTech

1. **Support all 3 major methods**: MoMo, ZaloPay, VietQR
2. **One-tap checkout**: Minimize friction for subscription renewals
3. **Installment options**: Partner with wallets offering "buy now pay later"
4. **Subscription reminders**: Pre-due date notifications via Zalo/WhatsApp
5. **Receipt automation**: Instant email/SMS confirmation

- **Successful platforms**: 8-12% with optimized onboarding
- **Key conversion triggers**: Accountability features (streaks, reminders), certificates/credentials, dependency on saved progress

**What Converts in Vietnam:**
1. **Accountability tools**: Progress tracking, parent reports, study schedules
2. **Certificate value**: Credentials recognized by schools/employers
3. **Data dependency**: Users who've built study history convert to protect it

5. **Social features**: Private study groups, peer competition

**What FAILS:**

- Paywalls on basic educational info (creates backlash)
- Ads removal as primary value proposition

3. **Support MoMo + ZaloPay + VietQR** from day one

**For B2B EdTech:**

3. **White-label strategy** leverages local brand trust
4. **Vietnamese language support** is non-negotiable
