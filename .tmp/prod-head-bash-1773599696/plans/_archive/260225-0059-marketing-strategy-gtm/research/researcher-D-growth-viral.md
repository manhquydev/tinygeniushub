## Viral Loop Patterns in EdTech

**Duolingo streak model** — anxiety-driven retention + social sharing. Streak milestones prompt organic social posts. "Year in Review" (Spotify Wrapped clone) drives massive Dec sharing. K-factor est. 0.7–0.9.

**ClassDojo B2B2C loop** — teacher invites parents → parents engage daily → parents share class stories to Facebook/WhatsApp → other parents download. Reached 85%+ US K-8 schools with near-zero paid marketing. K-factor ~0.8–1.1.

**Core viral loop types:**
1. **Pride Share Loop**: Child achieves → app celebrates → parent notified → parent shares → other parents see → download
2. **Competitive Loop**: Leaderboards → invite friends to compete → organic acquisition
3. **Classroom-to-Home Flywheel**: School adopts → teacher uses → student wants at home → parent pays for premium
4. **WhatsApp Cascade**: One parent asks "good math app?" → trusted chain recommendation in class group

**Weekly report as viral moment** — our email report is a dormant pride-share trigger. Adding one-tap "share my child's progress" to WhatsApp/Facebook transforms passive notification into acquisition channel.

## Referral Program Benchmarks

| Metric | EdTech Avg | Top Performers |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| Referral invite → signup | 15–25% | 30–45% |
| Signup → paid conversion | 20–35% | 40–60% |
| K-factor (viral coefficient) | 0.15–0.35 | 0.7–1.1 |
| Organic % of new users | 40–60% | >80% |
| Time to first referral sent | 14–21 days | 5–10 days |

**Incentive structure benchmarks:**
- Free month/extension: +45–65% conversion lift — best for subscription EdTech
- Cash/gift card ($10–25): +30–50% — higher friction, lower trust
- Tuition credit: +55–75% — works for high-ticket (tutoring, bootcamps)
- Feature unlock: +20–35% — freemium platforms

**Best practice**: Dual-sided incentives (both referrer + referee rewarded) outperform one-sided 2–3x. Offer "free week of premium" not % discount — parents respond better to experiencing the full product.

**Optimal trigger timing**: 3–7 days post-signup (after first "aha moment"), not at signup.

**Referral channel mix**: Direct link 35–45%, Social 20–30%, WhatsApp/SMS 15–25%, Email 10–20%.

## Community-Led Growth

**School/nursery B2B2C partnerships** are the dominant low-CAC acquisition model:
- B2B2C CAC is 40–60% lower than pure D2C
- School-endorsed apps see 3–5x higher parent conversion rates
- Teacher recommendation = authority transfer; removes "is this safe/effective?" barrier entirely

**Partnership playbook:**
1. Seed: 10–20 pilot nurseries/schools, teacher onboarding <10 min
2. Build parent invitation flow triggered by teacher setup
3. Design home-school continuity as core value prop (parents need the app to extend school learning)
4. "Summer retention" hook: convert school-year users to paid subscribers June–Aug
5. Compound: school case studies → district deals → school-to-school WOM

**Parent community building:**
- WhatsApp parent groups = #1 organic discovery channel (overtook Facebook globally by 2025)
- 73% of parents discover kids' apps through other parents vs. 18% via ads
- Freemium school-free / family-paid converts at 8–15% vs. 2–5% cold D2C
- Create a dedicated parent community space (Facebook Group, Zalo group for VN) with weekly tips

## Shareable Moments Strategy

**Design for "pride moments" — parents share outcomes, not features.**

High-shareability triggers:
- Weekly progress report card with child's photo + stats (current email system → add share button)
- Achievement badges at milestone completions (first lesson, 7-day streak, level-up)
- "Certificate of completion" for course/module — LinkedIn-ready format
- Personalized "Month in Review" auto-generated summary (Spotify Wrapped mechanic)
- "Child has learned X new things this week" with visual progress bar

**Technical requirements for viral sharing:**
- One-tap share to WhatsApp, Facebook, Zalo (VN priority), Instagram Stories
- Pre-filled caption: "Con tôi vừa [achievement] trên CùngConTuHọc! 🎉"
- Image optimized for WhatsApp (square, <1MB, bold text visible without expanding)
- Track share events → measure downstream installs (UTM on share links)

**Weekly report upgrade** (highest-impact quick win):
- Add child avatar/photo to report
- Include 1 shareable achievement highlight card
- Add "Share con's progress" CTA button in email
- Generate unique referral link embedded in share

## Word-of-Mouth Triggers

**What makes parents recommend to other parents:**

1. **Visible child progress** — "My kid learned X in Y weeks" — observable outcome is the recommendation. Track and surface this metric prominently.
2. **Emotional relief / guilt removal** — replaces "bad screen time" with educational time. Position as solution to parenting anxiety.
3. **Good parent signaling** — recommending EdTech signals conscious parenting. High in competitive school environments.
4. **Peer validation loops** — one parent asks in WhatsApp group → 3–5 parents vouch → 10 parents download. Design for this scenario.
5. **Teacher/school endorsement** — single most powerful trust signal. One teacher recommending = 30+ parent installs.
6. **Shared context** — parents in same school have identical problems. Hyper-local targeting (one school at a time) beats broad demographics.

**Framing that travels in parent WOM:**
- Outcome-first: "X learned to read in 3 months"
- Problem-solution: "Stops screen time guilt"
- Social proof: "All the kids in [School Name] use it"
- Authority: "Recommended by [Teacher Name]"

## Key Growth Metrics

| Metric | Target (Early Stage) | World-Class |
|--------|---------------------|-------------|
| Activation rate (complete first session) | >40% | >70% |
| D7 retention | >25% | >45% |
| D30 retention | >15% | >30% |
| K-factor (viral coefficient) | >0.3 | >0.7 |
| Referral rate (% users who refer) | >10% | >25% |
| Weekly report open rate | >40% | >60% |
| Weekly report share rate | >5% (new metric) | >15% |
| Parent DAU/MAU | >20% | >50% |
| Referral → signup conversion | >20% | >40% |
| School → parent conversion | >30% | >60% |

**K-factor formula**: K = (avg invites sent per user) × (invite → signup conversion rate)
K > 1.0 = viral growth (self-sustaining). K = 0.5 means each user brings 0.5 new users on average.

**Priority metrics for our weekly report system:**
- Report open rate (baseline)
- Share CTA click rate (new)
- Installs from shared reports (referral attribution)
- Report-open → app-open same day (re-activation)
