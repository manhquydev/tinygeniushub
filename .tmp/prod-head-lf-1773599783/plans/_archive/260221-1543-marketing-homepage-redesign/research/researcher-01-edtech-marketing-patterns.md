# Research: EdTech Landing Page Conversion & Vietnamese Parent UX

## 1. High-Converting EdTech Landing Page Structure

### Optimal Section Order (Conversion Funnel)
1. **Hero** — benefit-driven headline, single primary CTA, social proof snippet
2. **Pain/Problem** — empathize with frustration (video babysitting, no progress visibility)
3. **Solution/How It Works** — 3-4 step visual flow, simple and scannable
4. **Features as Benefits** — frame features as parent outcomes, not tech specs
5. **Social Proof** — testimonials with specific outcomes ("con tôi đã biết đếm 1-20 sau 2 tuần")
6. **Product Demo** — screenshots/GIFs showing real UI (dashboard, kid mode, weekly report)
7. **Pricing** — transparent, low-friction, emphasize trial = no credit card
8. **Trust Signals** — security, refund policy, data privacy
9. **FAQ** — objection handling disguised as helpful answers
10. **Final CTA** — urgency + benefit reminder, mirror hero CTA

### Copy Patterns That Convert
- **Pain → Agitate → Solve**: "Cho con xem video cả ngày nhưng không biết con học được gì?"
- **Specificity**: "15 phút mỗi ngày" beats "học mỗi ngày"
- **Outcome-focused**: "Thấy rõ con tiến bộ" > "Hệ thống theo dõi tiến độ"
- **Social proof numbers**: "2,000+ phụ huynh đã tin tưởng" (even pre-launch, use aspirational)

## 2. Vietnamese Parent Demographics & UX

### Mobile Usage
- 70%+ Vietnamese internet users access via mobile
- Parents 25-40 are primary smartphone users
- Touch targets minimum 44px, thumb-friendly bottom CTAs
- Page speed critical on 4G networks — aim for <3s LCP

### Trust Signals for VN Market
- Vietnamese parents prioritize: educational credentials, community validation, transparent pricing in VND
- "Không cần thẻ tín dụng" is a strong trust signal (credit card penetration still low in VN)
- Refund policy + clear cancellation = reduces anxiety about recurring charges
- Vietnamese names in testimonials (not Western names) build authenticity

### Pricing Presentation
- Show VND prominently (not USD)
- Price anchoring: show per-day cost ("chỉ 329đ/ngày" for Standard yearly)
- Trial emphasis: "7 ngày miễn phí, không cần thẻ"
- Comparison table for Standard vs Family+ (keep simple, max 5 rows)

## 3. Pre-Launch Social Proof Strategies
- Use realistic Vietnamese parent personas with specific outcomes
- Include child age for relatability ("Bé Minh, 4 tuổi")
- Mix demographics: working mom, stay-at-home dad, grandparent caregiver
- Star ratings (4.8-4.9 range feels authentic, 5.0 feels fake)
- "Đang dùng thử" / "Đã dùng 3 tháng" adds time context

## 4. Recommendations for Cung Con Tu Hoc Homepage

| Element | Recommendation |
|---------|---------------|
| Headline | Pain/gain: "Mỗi ngày 15 phút, phụ huynh thấy rõ con tiến bộ" |
| Sub-headline | Agitate: "Không còn lo con xem video cả ngày mà không học được gì" |
| Primary CTA | "Dùng thử 7 ngày miễn phí" (green, full-width on mobile) |

| Pain points | 3 specific frustrations with icons |
| How it works | 4 steps with numbered icons |
| Testimonials | 3 cards, Vietnamese names, specific outcomes |
| Pricing | 2-column comparison, per-day cost highlighted |
| FAQ | 5-6 questions, accordion style |
| Final CTA | "Bắt đầu hành trình học tập cùng con" |
