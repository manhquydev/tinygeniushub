# Researcher B: Business Model & Pricing Strategy

## Model Comparison (SaaS vs Freemium vs Pay-Per-Course)

| Metric | Freemium | Subscription | Pay-Per-Course |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free→Paid conversion | 1–8% (consumer) | 10–25% (trial-gated) | 1–15% |
| Avg LTV | $50–$200 | $120–$360 (consumer) | $80–$300 |
| CAC payback | 12–24 mo | 6–18 mo | 1–6 mo |
| Revenue predictability | Low | High | Low–Medium |
| Churn risk | High | Medium | Low (transactional) |

**Freemium**: Needs massive scale to work. Duolingo converts ~7% of huge free base. Only viable with viral loops at scale.

**Subscription (annual)**: Best fit for habit-based EdTech. Annual plan = 3–5x lower churn than monthly. Predictable MRR.

**Pay-per-course**: Fastest CAC payback; good for catalog validation but low LTV ceiling.

**Recommendation for cungcontuhoc**: Annual subscription + freemium discovery tier. At $5–10/yr price point, focus on volume over ARPU. Freemium serves as top-of-funnel; annual sub is the monetization layer.

## Free Trial Benchmarks

| Trial Length | Conversion Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 7-day | 15–25% | High urgency; works if "aha moment" is immediate |
| 14-day | 18–28% | Sweet spot — best performing across most SaaS (ProfitWell) |
| 30-day | 10–18% | Procrastination effect; users disengage by day 7–10 |

**Key insight**: Trial length matters less than triggering the "aha moment" within 72 hours. 40–60% of trial users never log in a second time (Totango).

**For early childhood EdTech**: 7-day is ideal — parents see child engagement fast. Pair with daily nudge emails/notifications. Credit-card-required trials convert 40–60% vs 18–25% opt-in, but destroy top-of-funnel volume; avoid for Vietnamese consumer market (low trust for CC entry).

**Recommendation**: 7-day free trial, no CC required, strong Day 1–3 onboarding sequence.

## Annual vs Monthly Psychology

**Pricing psychology principles:**

1. **Anchoring**: Show monthly equivalent of annual ("chỉ 10,000 VND/tháng") — makes price feel trivial
2. **Loss framing**: "Tiết kiệm 40%" outperforms "2 months free" in SEA markets (value-conscious audience)
3. **Default bias**: Pre-selecting annual on pricing toggle increases annual uptake 10–40% (CXL, Unbounce A/B data)
4. **Pain of paying reduction**: Annual = 1 payment event vs 12; subscribers use product more, churn less
5. **Commitment/consistency** (Cialdini): Annual payers justify purchase, form habits, renew

**Discount norms by segment:**

| Segment | Typical Annual Discount |
|---|---|
| B2B SaaS | 15–20% |
| Consumer EdTech (global) | 30–50% |
| Vietnam consumer (price-sensitive) | 30–50% (essential to convert) |

**cungcontuhoc math**: Monthly 20,000 VND × 12 = 240,000/yr. Annual at 120,000 = 50% off. This is aggressive but appropriate for Vietnam's price sensitivity and positions annual as the clear "smart" choice.

## Upsell Mechanics

**Free → Paid triggers (what actually works):**

1. **Streak/progress lock**: "Bé đã học được 7 ngày liên tiếp — mở khóa thêm để tiếp tục!" (loss aversion)
2. **Content wall at engagement peak**: Hit paywall after child completes 3–5 free lessons (not day 1)
3. **Parent progress report**: Show learning milestones in free tier; gate detailed reports behind paid
4. **Social proof at friction point**: "95% phụ huynh tiếp tục sau 7 ngày dùng thử"
5. **Timer/urgency**: Limited-time annual discount (real deadlines only — fake countdowns erode trust)

**Basic → Premium triggers:**

1. **Feature discovery**: Surface premium features contextually (not upfront) — when parent would naturally want them
2. **Child milestone**: "Con bạn sẵn sàng cho cấp độ tiếp theo" → upgrade CTA
3. **Sibling accounts**: "Thêm 1 tài khoản trẻ em chỉ +30,000 VND/năm"
4. **Offline access**: Premium unlock for travel/no-internet use cases

**Duolingo's proven model** (applicable): Streak freeze, heart system, ad-free, offline — each targets a specific pain point the free user has already experienced. Map friction → monetize that friction.

## LTV/CAC Benchmarks (EdTech SEA)

**CAC ranges (Vietnam/SEA, bootstrapped):**

| Channel | CAC (VND) | CAC (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Paid social (Facebook/TikTok) | 80,000–300,000 | $3–$12 |
| Organic / SEO / content | 20,000–80,000 | $1–$3 |
| Referral / word-of-mouth | 10,000–40,000 | $0.5–$2 |
| Zalo/Facebook community | 10,000–50,000 | $0.5–$2 |

**LTV at $5–10/yr price point:**

- Year 1 LTV: 120,000–240,000 VND ($5–10)
- Year 2 renewal (est. 50–60% retention): adds $2.5–6
- 3-year LTV: ~$10–18 per customer

**LTV:CAC targets:**

| Stage | LTV:CAC | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Year 1 bootstrapped | 1.5x–2.5x | Acceptable while funnel learning |
| Healthy target | 3x+ | Sustainable growth |
| Excellent | 5x+ | Organic/community-dominant |

**Key**: At $5–10/yr ARPU, CAC MUST stay below $3–5 to hit 3x LTV:CAC. This makes organic, referral, and community channels non-optional. Paid ads only viable if LTV increases via upsell (sibling accounts, higher tiers).

**Revenue levers to accelerate:**
1. Increase ARPU: Add family plan (2 children) at 180,000–200,000 VND/yr (50% more revenue, ~80% same CAC)
2. School/kindergarten B2B tier: 1 school = 20–100 families; CAC efficiency 10x
3. Annual-only promotion in Q4 (back-to-school Sept, Tết Jan–Feb): seasonal conversion spikes
4. Referral program: "Giới thiệu 1 bạn, nhận 1 tháng miễn phí" — zero marginal CAC
